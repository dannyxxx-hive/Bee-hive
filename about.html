About this journal

Marginalia started as a note-taking habit that outgrew its notebook. Most weeks there's one short piece — never reported, never optimized for anything, just an honest attempt to describe something before the impression of it fades.

The name comes from the notes people used to leave in the margins of books: half-formed, a little private, more interesting for being unfinished. That's the standard here. Nothing on this site is trying to convince you of anything.

If a post is useful to you, that's a bonus. If it's just a record that someone once paid close attention to an ordinary Tuesday, that's enough too.
